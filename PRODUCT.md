# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

ARMM manages Arma Reforger Workshop mods in user-owned modsets, compares installed and latest versions, handles dependencies, exports server JSON, and sends scoped Discord notifications.

## Users

Modset owners, group members, administrators and authenticated service accounts. Administrators have emergency access to all modsets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve permissions, dependency safeguards, export ordering and upgrade compatibility. English interface. Existing React/TypeScript and FastAPI stack. Development containers may be updated for review.

## Brand Commitments

Keep dark neutral surfaces and yellow/gold accent, the ARMM logo and existing light mode. No decorative glass or blur.

## Open Decisions

Desktop workflows are the working priority; the balance of desktop and mobile usage remains unconfirmed.
